PROSCAN OPERATOR'S GUIDE

1. Primary Control Rows (Top 6 Buttons)

The primary controls at the top are arranged into two dedicated rows: AUDIO and DISPLAY on their own row at the very top, followed by LOG IN, LOG OUT, BRAND, and GUIDE on the row directly beneath:

  • AUDIO Connects or disconnects the live scanner audio stream. Its inline LED turns green when active and enables real-time audio modulation detection.
  • DISPLAY Powers the unified scanner screen, 24-hour clock, and integrated live history log ON or OFF in unison. Turning it ON randomly triggers one of 6 synchronized hardware start-up boot animations: AV1 / SCART, RF TUNE // LOCK, LCD SELF-TEST 8888, CRT RASTER SCAN, CAPACITOR WARMUP, or V-HOLD SYNC. Turning it OFF immediately cuts the screen to pure black across all themes and collapses it into a crisp white SCART CRT horizontal line and phosphor center dot.
  • LOG IN Opens the Super User password prompt so you can remotely control scanner keys. Viewing the display, listening to audio, and changing themes work without logging in.
  • LOG OUT Ends your active ProScan Super User control session.
  • BRAND Opens the Custom Brand dialog to personalise the centre faceplate badge (up to 10 characters, automatically followed by RADIOS). If a custom brand is already active, tapping BRAND immediately removes it.
  • GUIDE Opens or closes this comprehensive reference guide at any time.

2. Top Status Bar & Hardware LEDs

  • WAKE Slide Switch & Blue Pulsating LED: Keeps your mobile or tablet screen awake continuously so the device never locks while monitoring.
  • RX Orange LED (Smart Beep Filter): Illuminates automatically when genuine voice or data radio transmissions are received on the live audio stream, ignoring short single-tone keypad beeps.
  • LOG Purple Double-Flash LED: Flashes purple whenever a new transmission row is logged in the History Log table (or when previewing Log FX).

3. Display Colours & Surface Textures (Hold DISP Colour Button)

Tap DISP: [COLOUR] to cycle through 8 main display colour themes (SOFT, LCD GREEN, BRT GREEN, LCD BLUE, LCD AMBER, OLED BRT, TERM, and PAPER). Press and hold (0.5s long press with haptic vibration) on the DISP: [COLOUR] button to cycle through 10 screen surface textures:

  • RETRO CRT: Horizontal cathode scanlines with a recessed tube vignette.
  • DOT MATRIX: Classic 3×3px dot-matrix grid with crisp pixelated rendering.
  • PIXEL GRID: Fine 4×4px square LCD matrix grid.
  • VFD GRILLE: Vacuum fluorescent vertical filament wires and fine mesh.
  • EMI MESH: Cross-hatched RF shielded cockpit glass weave.
  • GLOSS GLASS: Specular angled glass reflection over the display.
  • SMOOTH: Clean, untextured high-clarity display surface.
  • CRACKED LCD: Shattered spiderweb glass fracture lines with a dark liquid-crystal bruise—procedurally randomized every time it is selected.
  • BAD CONTACT: Smooth screen with a randomized loose-ribbon fault simulator that periodically glitches the readout, clock, log, and scrollbar.
  • CRACK + GLITCH: Combines a freshly randomized CRACKED LCD fracture pattern with BAD CONTACT random glitches.

4. Scanner Operations Keypad

  • HOLD / RES Holds on the current channel/frequency or resumes scanning.
  • L / O Locks out (skips) or unlocks the active channel/frequency.
  • SCAN Enters main bank/channel scan mode.
  • V - / V + Adjusts scanner audio volume down or up.
  • LIMIT SRCH Initiates a Limit Search sequence automatically.
  • SQ - / SQ + Adjusts scanner squelch threshold down or up.
  • SCROLL ⬇ / SCROLL ⬆ Steps down or up through channels, frequencies, or menu options.
  • DIRECT Enters Direct frequency/channel entry mode.
  • MENU / BACK Opens the scanner settings menu or steps back.

5. Text Colours, 24 Realistic Skins, Hit Counter & Log FX

  • Frequency Hit Counter: The live log automatically tallies how many times each frequency has been logged in History Log.txt and displays a compact badge (e.g. ×4) next to repeat channels.
  • TXT: [COLOUR] Short Tap cycles the Main Display Text Colour (AUTO, SOFT, WHITE, GREEN, AMBER, CYAN, INK, RED). Long Press (0.5s with haptic vibration) independently cycles the Log & Clock Text Colour through the same palette.
  • FX: [EFFECT] Cycles how new log rows animate in (with an instant preview on the top row): BLOCK, SCROLL, BOARD, TYPE, GLOW, and OFF.
  • DISP: 100% Cycles display brightness through 100%, 80%, 60%, 40%, and 20%.
  • SKIN: [NAME] Cycles through 24 hardware chassis skins, including realistic CARBON, TITAN, PEBBLE, WOOD, TACTIC, and COPPER finishes.
  • BG: [NAME] Cycles the outer environment backdrop through 8 styles.

6. Numeric Keypad & 10-Slot Favourite Presets (0–9)

Numeric keys 0–9 serve as both scanner keypad buttons and 10 favourite theme preset slots (with haptic vibration feedback on long press):

  • Short Tap (0–9, . / NO, E / YES): Sends standard numeric digits, decimal/No, or Enter/Yes to the scanner.
  • Long Press (0.5s) on an Empty Key (0–9): Saves your current complete look to that number (FAV X: SAVED).
  • Long Press (0.5s) on a Saved Key (0–9): Loads that saved favourite (FAV X: LOADED).
  • Keep Holding (3.0s) on a Saved Key (0–9): Overwrites that slot with your current theme combination (FAV X: OVERWRITTEN!).
